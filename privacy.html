Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

"OpenClaw" ("the app") is a personal Google OAuth app used only by its owner, Nazrin, with her own Google account. It is not the official OpenClaw project.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app can access the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Apps Script, Tasks, Contacts and basic profile (name and e-mail address).

How the data is used

Data is used only to perform actions the owner asks her own AI assistants to do, such as showing recent e-mails, creating a spreadsheet or uploading a backup file to her Drive. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and private server, with access restricted to the owner. Content read from Google services is processed on demand and is not copied to any other service, except that the owner's AI assistant providers process the request text needed to answer her.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are then deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
