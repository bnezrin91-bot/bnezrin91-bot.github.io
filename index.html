OpenClaw - personal Google Workspace connector

"OpenClaw" is the name of a personal, single-user Google OAuth app owned by Nazrin (GitHub: bnezrin91-bot). It lets her own AI assistants (Claude Code on her computer and her self-hosted OpenClaw agent on her private server) work with her own Google account: read and send Gmail, manage Calendar events, and work with Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms and Apps Script.

It also uploads encrypted nightly backups of the owner's server to her own Google Drive.

This is a personal app. It is not the official OpenClaw project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public, and no other Google accounts are connected to it.

How it works

The assistant uses the open-source Google Workspace MCP server. Sign-in uses Google OAuth; the access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and server.
